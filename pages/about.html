Welcome

About Liberia

Liberia is a country on the west coast of Africa known for its beautiful coastline, tropical forests, rich cultural heritage, and welcoming communities.

From the energy of Monrovia to the beaches of Robertsport, the landscapes of Mount Nimba, and communities across the country, Liberia offers travelers many different experiences.

Our Story

A Rich History

Liberia has a distinctive place in African history. The country declared independence in 1847 and became the first independent republic in Africa.

Its history has shaped its cities, communities, traditions, architecture, and national identity.

Wild & Beautiful

Liberia contains important forests, rivers, wetlands, mountains, and coastal ecosystems.

Its natural landscapes provide opportunities for exploration, photography, wildlife experiences, hiking, beaches, and responsible ecotourism.
