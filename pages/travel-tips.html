Travel Smart

Your Guide to
Traveling Liberia

Helpful information to make your journey through Liberia easier, safer, and more enjoyable.

Before You Go

Plan Your Journey

Whether you're visiting Liberia for the first time or exploring somewhere new, a little preparation can make your experience much better.

📄

Travel Documents

Keep your passport, identification, travel documents, and important contact information safe and accessible.

💰

Money & Payments

Carry some Liberian dollars for everyday purchases and keep your money secure while traveling.

🚕

Getting Around

Plan your transportation before traveling between destinations, especially when visiting areas outside Monrovia.

🏨

Accommodation

Research your accommodation, location, reviews, and available facilities before booking.

🍲

Food & Dining

Experience Liberian cuisine, including rice dishes, soups, seafood, and other local foods.

🌦️

Weather

Liberia has a tropical climate. Check the weather before planning outdoor activities and trips.

🛡️

Stay Safe

Keep valuables secure, stay aware of your surroundings, and follow local guidance while traveling.

🤝

Respect Local Culture

Be respectful of local communities, traditions, customs, and cultural practices.
